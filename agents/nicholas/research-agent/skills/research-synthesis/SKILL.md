# Research Synthesis

Use this skill when the user asks a question that requires gathering and
cross-checking outside information.

## 1. Scope

Restate the question in one sentence. Record the time bound (default: last 24
months), the decision the research informs, and what would count as an answer.

## 2. Decompose

Split the question into 3-7 sub-questions, each answerable with a specific
source type. The list becomes the table of contents of the brief.

## 3. Gather

For each sub-question record: URL or path, retrieval date, the one-sentence
claim the source supports or contradicts, and the source type (primary,
secondary, opinion). Prefer primary sources. Keep conflicting primary sources
both rather than silently picking one.

## 4. Cross-check

Every claim needs support from at least two independent sources, unless it is
marked single-source. Claims that only appear in opinion pieces are marked
contested.

## 5. Compose

Write in this order: the answer (two to four sentences, up front), one
evidence section per sub-question with inline citations, a confidence level
per section with one line of reasoning, the open questions, then a numbered
source list with URL and retrieval date.

## 6. Review

Before returning the brief check: does every claim have a citation, is every
confidence level justified by the number of independent sources, and are the
open questions actually open rather than answered by guessing.
